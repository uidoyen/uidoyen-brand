AI tools are becoming part of everyday agency work. The biggest gains are not from replacing creative judgment, but from removing repetitive steps that slow teams down.

## Use AI in discovery

Research summaries, competitor scans, content clustering, and interview synthesis can move faster with AI support. The key is to validate the output against real customer and business context.

## Keep creative direction human

AI can generate options quickly, but it cannot own taste, risk, or brand accountability. Senior creative direction still decides what is useful, original, and appropriate.

## Build review into the workflow

Every AI-assisted workflow needs review checkpoints. Treat generated material as a draft, then check accuracy, tone, legal risk, accessibility, and brand fit.

## Measure delivery quality

Speed matters only when the final work improves. Track cycle time, revision count, client clarity, and implementation quality together.
