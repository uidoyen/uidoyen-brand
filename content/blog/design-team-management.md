Great design teams need more than talent. They need shared standards, practical rituals, and enough clarity to make strong decisions without constant approval loops.

## Define quality

Teams move faster when they know what good work means. Document principles for usability, visual craft, accessibility, performance, and brand consistency.

## Make critique specific

Useful critique connects feedback to user goals and business outcomes. Vague preferences slow teams down and make decisions harder to repeat.

## Protect focus time

Design requires deep work. Reduce unnecessary meetings, keep feedback consolidated, and give teams enough context before asking for solutions.

## Clarify ownership

Every project should have a clear decision maker. Ownership helps teams move through tradeoffs without losing momentum.
