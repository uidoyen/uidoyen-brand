UX pain usually appears when product intent and user expectations drift apart. Teams can avoid many issues by reviewing the experience from the user's point of view early and often.

## Unclear next steps

Users should always understand what happened, what they can do next, and what the system needs from them. Ambiguous flows create hesitation and support requests.

## Too much visual noise

When everything competes for attention, nothing feels important. Clear hierarchy helps users scan, decide, and act without unnecessary effort.

## Weak empty states

Empty states, errors, and loading screens are part of the product experience. Treat them as moments to build trust, not as afterthoughts.

## Missing feedback loops

Projects improve faster when teams watch real sessions, review support data, and test assumptions before investing in large interface changes.
