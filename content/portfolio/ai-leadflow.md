AI LeadFlow replaces manual sales outreach with an intelligent multi-channel lead qualification system. Built around automated WhatsApp and email messaging, it qualifies inbound leads instantly, collects requirement details, and updates the CRM in real-time.

The system ensures zero lead drop-off by sending personalized follow-ups, scheduling meetings automatically, and routing high-value opportunities directly to account executives.
