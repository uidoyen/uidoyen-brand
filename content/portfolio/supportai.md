SupportAI empowers customer success teams by integrating a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) knowledge engine directly into live chat and support channels.

It accurately answers complex customer queries using internal documentation, resolves repetitive support tickets autonomously, and escalates edge cases with rich context to human agents.
