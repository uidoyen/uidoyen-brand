DocuFlow AI automates the extraction of structured data from complex invoices, contracts, and receipts using advanced multi-modal AI vision and LLM parsing.

It eliminates manual data entry, validates extracted information against business logic rules, and seamlessly syncs formatted data directly into internal ERPs and financial databases.
